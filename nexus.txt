Strips custom colors, font sizes, and rainbow text formatting from chat messages while keeping default system text intact.

[b]General[/b]
- Strip Custom Colors
- Strip Custom Fonts
- Strip Custom Sizes
- Strip Text Decorations (Underline & Strikethrough)
- Force All Lowercase
